# 0003 — Fictional world instead of real history

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Topic:** 01 — World and setting
- **Amends:** [0002](0002-design-pillars.md) (pillar 3 name)

## Context

The initial pitch placed the game in real history (c. 1400–1600). Topic 00 then set
the rule "all rulers are engineers", which real history cannot support. A real
setting also demands heavy research and invites accuracy disputes.

## Options considered

1. Real history — strongest hook for history fans; conflicts with the ruler-engineer
   rule; heavy research.
2. Light alternate history — real geography and states, one divergence; still heavy
   research.
3. Fictional world with real physics — full freedom over map and states; the
   historical hook is lost.

## Decision

Option 3. The world is fictional, with **nothing supernatural**: invented states,
cultures and myths, but real-world physics and military engineering. The technology
level matches the real gunpowder era, and the game begins when tall walls are still
standard and cannon are only just becoming effective (≈ 1494 equivalent). The map is
hand-crafted and inspired by Renaissance Italy.

Pillar 3 in ADR 0002 is renamed from "You rediscover history" to
**"You rediscover the revolution"**; its meaning is unchanged.

## Consequences

- The pitch, the realism rule and the secondary audience in 00-vision are reworded
  (history → real-world physics and military engineering).
- No historical figures, events or sieges. Scenarios become fictional engineering
  challenges (not in the MVP).
- Real fortification and artillery literature stays relevant as a reference for
  physics and design, not for events.
- The map, states, names and lore must be invented.
