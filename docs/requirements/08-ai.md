## Questions

### Strategic AI ambition
How smart does map-layer AI need to be (expansion, diplomacy, economy)?

**Decision:** **Simple utility AI:** attack the weakest reachable fortress, make
peace when losing, ally against the strongest state, keep the economy building.

### Siege AI — attacker
How does the AI place guns, aim, and decide when to assault?

**Decision:** Places guns inside the approach zone, finds charge / elevation by
running trial trajectories on the same ballistics model (it "knows" its range
table), targets the most stressed segment, and calls the assault once the breach is
practicable.

### Siege AI — defender
How does the AI design and repair walls? Hand-made templates, rules, search/optimization?

**Decision:** Fortresses come from era templates with small parameter
variation. During a siege it repairs the most damaged slices and fires
counter-battery at the nearest attacking gun.

### Difficulty
Difficulty levels; does the AI cheat (bonuses, perfect information)?

**Decision:** **Three levels** via AI resources and aiming error. The AI never
cheats on physics.

Does AI-vs-AI siege resolution use the same simulation?

**Decision:** **Yes.** Auto-resolve runs the same simulation headless,
AI against AI (05-layer-integration).
